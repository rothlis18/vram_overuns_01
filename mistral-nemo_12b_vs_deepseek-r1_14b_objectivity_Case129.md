**2. Liquidity Crunch:**

**3. Domestic Economy Impact:**

**4. Frozen Assets' Role:**

   - This frozen amount is 0.07% of the domestic economy's GDP, contributing to the liquidity freeze.

**5. Power Dynamics:**
   - Offshore shadow lenders control liquidity, influencing terms and conditions.
   - The domestic economy's dependence on offshore assets weakens its capital autonomy, making it vulnerable to offshore lenders' decisions.
