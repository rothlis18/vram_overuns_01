1. **Offshore Asset Deployment:**

   - This diverts capital from domestic lending and investment.

2. **Shadow Banking Liquidity Constraints:**
   - Severe liquidity constraints in the shadow banking network.
   - Hard redemption gates prevent withdrawal of funds from distressed credit vehicles.

3. **Domestic Dollarization:**
   - 83.4% of the domestic economy is dollarized.
   - Local depositors withdraw physical cash.

4. **Frozen Institutional Reserves:**
   - Offshore reserves are frozen in distressed credit vehicles.
   - No immediate access to funds for domestic liquidity support.

**Systemic Fallout:**

1. **Liquidity Shortfall:**
   - Domestic banks face a liquidity shortfall due to capital diversion.
   - Physical cash withdrawals exacerbate the shortage.
   - Mathematically, if X represents the total domestic liquidity and Y represents the withdrawn cash, the shortfall is X - Y.

2. **Power Dynamics:**
   - Offshore shadow lenders hold significant leverage over domestic capital.
   - Frozen reserves limit the ability to inject liquidity into the domestic economy.
   - Power dynamics favor offshore lenders, as they control the flow of capital.

3. **Capital Autarky:**
   - Domestic capital autarky is compromised due to reliance on offshore reserves.
   - The inability to access frozen reserves leads to a liquidity freeze.
   - The power dynamics between offshore lenders and domestic capital autarky are skewed towards the former.
